What this installer does on your PC

1. Copies the app (about 1 MB) into the folder shown on the next page,
   inside your own user profile. No administrator rights are needed, and
   other users of this PC are not affected.

2. Adds YT Audio Extractor to your Start menu, and to your desktop if you
   choose to.

What happens the first time you open the app

A window opens and shows each step as it happens:

- It downloads three helper programs into the app's own "bin" folder,
  about 160 MB in total:
    Python 3.14   a private copy, used only by this app and separate
                  from any Python already on your PC
    ffmpeg        converts audio between formats and writes the tags and
                  cover art
    Deno          solves YouTube's JavaScript challenges
  Each download is checked against a pinned SHA-256 fingerprint before it
  is used.
- It installs two Python packages into that private Python: Flask (runs
  the app's page) and yt-dlp (the engine that talks to YouTube).
- It opens the app in your web browser at http://localhost:5000. The page
  runs on your own PC; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Later launches skip the downloads and start in a few seconds. Each launch
checks for a newer yt-dlp, because YouTube changes often.

Nothing is installed system-wide: no PATH changes and no background
services. Besides your audio files, the app writes only two small files:
your last-used folder (in %APPDATA%\YT-Audio-Extractor) and a download
history (in Downloads\YT-Audio).

Uninstalling (Settings > Apps, or the Start menu) removes the app and
everything it downloaded. Your audio files, the settings file and the
download history are kept.
